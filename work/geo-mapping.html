Our work / Data visualisation & maps / Geo mapping

Maps

Geo mapping

Relief maps, trade-flow maps and interactive choropleths – from coal power plants to China's net exports by province.

Client
Energy & commodities research
Direction
Data visualisation & maps
Format
Maps
Tags
Maps · Energy · Trade

Challenge

Energy and commodities research often depends on geography: where power plants are, how coal and other goods are traded between regions, how indicators differ by province. These stories need maps, not tables.

Approach

  1. Used shaded-relief regional maps to place assets such as coal power plants in their landscape.
  2. Built global trade-flow maps to show the direction and size of trade between countries.
  3. Published interactive choropleths in Datawrapper, for example China's net exports by province, so readers can explore the data themselves.

What we delivered

  • Shaded-relief regional maps.
  • Global trade-flow maps.
  • Interactive Datawrapper maps for online publication.

Outcome

Geographic data from the research became maps that work in print reports and as interactive graphics online.

Materials

  • Coal companies and thermal power plants (interactive)
  • China's net exports by province (interactive)
  • Installed capacity of India's coal power plants by region, MW (interactive)
  • Total generation (GWh), 2022 (interactive)
